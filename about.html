ENTERPRISE PROFILE

POWERING HEAVY INDUSTRY & INFRASTRUCTURE

Sri Neya Crane Services (SNC) is an established heavy commercial lifting powerhouse headquartered at Kullanampatti Petrol Bunk, Ponnagaram, Natham Byepass, Dindigul.

OUR OPERATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

ENGINEERING EVERY LIFT WITH UNCOMPROMISING PRECISION

Founded to provide world-class heavy lifting solutions across Tamil Nadu, Sri Neya Crane Services has evolved from a local mobile crane provider to a multi-district industrial logistics and emergency rescue partner.

Whether executing a 30-meter high mast tower installation, maneuvering heavy factory presses into tight basement pits, or clearing emergency highway rollovers on NH-44, our team delivers unmatched speed, certified safety, and transparent pricing.

UNCOMPROMISING SAFETY PROTOCOLS

THE ZERO-INCIDENT GOLD STANDARD

Safety isn’t merely a policy at Sri Neya—it is the foundation of every boom extension, outrigger stabilization, and rigging hookup.

Daily 42-Point Pre-Lift Inspection

Rigorous daily verification of hydraulic fluid pressures, wire rope integrity, outrigger pad ground bearing pressures, and anti-two-block limit switches.

Certified Master Riggers

Our crane operators and ground signalmen possess multi-year industrial training, certified heavy vehicle endorsements, and mandatory PPE compliance.

Engineered CAD Lift Planning

For complex tandem lifts and oversized modules, our team prepares CAD rigging blueprints with load distributions, wind tolerances, and swing radii.
